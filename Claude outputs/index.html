Hand Movements for Energy Cultivation

  1. Rub the hands together briskly until you feel warmth and tingling — friction wakes the meridians in the palms.
  2. Open the hands to the sky, palms up, to draw down celestial energy.

  4. Speak the Dining Varessence:
"I plant the seeds of greatness, merging nutrient-dense sensations. I invite my seat of power woven in what I devour."
