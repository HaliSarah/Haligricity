Daily Practice

Three practice arcs — Rising, Zenith, and Astral. The body thrives on rhythm; this is the trellis.

Rising Ritual
Varessence · Bowls · Pranayama · Mudra · Qigong · Yoga · Lymph

Before you move — before you open your eyes fully, before you reach for your phone — stay in bed. Let your mind be still. In that quiet space between sleep and waking, follow any fragment of a dream back as far as it will go.

Then reach for your dream journal and record it: give the entry a title and a date. Over time, repeated symbols, repeated people, repeated settings will begin to emerge.

If possible, begin the rise with bare feet on the actual earth — grass, soil, sand, or stone. A grounding mat plugged into a properly grounded outlet works just as well indoors.

Once grounded — physically or energetically — hold space. Let yourself be still until you arrive at a place of genuine emptiness.

From this place of stillness, the Rising Varessence can land.

Hands together at the heart center. Speak the Rising Varessence aloud — repeat if needed, until you can feel it land in the body.

"I honor myself, on this day, and shine my light, along the way."

Strike or rim your singing bowls (brass or crystal) three times to clear the residue of the dreamworld. Begin with the highest note; when you reach the lowest, you have fully arrived back into your body.

Form Vishnu Mudra with your right hand. Surya Bhedana — the right-nostril solar breath — inhale through the right (solar) nostril, exhale through the left. Repeat for 5–10 rounds.

Pull your daily, weekly, or monthly mudra from a card deck. Hold the gesture for whatever feels right.

Meditating while holding the Mudra is encouraged. Let the gesture anchor you as you explore your goals for the day.

Move stagnant overnight energy and wake the meridian channels. Watch Rising Qigong →

Follow the Qigong with a sun salutation practice to build heat and anchor the body before the day begins.

Seal the rising practice with three gentle lymph drainages: Neck (30–40 sweeps toward the shoulder), Armpits (30–40 slides front to back), Tummy (36 counter-clockwise circles around the navel, then 24 off the skin).

Zenith Ritual
Physical Grounding · Varessence

Go outside and connect bare feet, bare hands, or both directly to the earth for at least five minutes.

The earth carries a mild negative charge. Research confirms earthing reduces inflammation, improves sleep, stabilizes cortisol rhythms, and calms the nervous system.

If you cannot go outside, use a grounding mat connected to a properly grounded outlet.

While grounded — feet or hands on the earth — speak the Zenith Varessence aloud:

"This celestial body holds me.
Wrapped in its natural rhythms closely,
I allow the exchange of ions.
Peace radiates from all my cells,
anchoring me in Zion."

Remain grounded for as long as feels right after speaking. Let the earth respond.

Before You Begin — Health Maintenance

Two hours after your last meal, complete your skincare routine and change into pajamas or yoga clothes.

Oral Care: Brush and floss. Hali Sarah uses a toothbrush with water only, silk dental floss and metal dental tools as needed, plus Jamaican toothbrushes (licorice sticks) and oral probiotics.

EMF Reduction: Switch your phone to airplane mode. Unplug WiFi when possible. — See orgone products for additional assistance.

Astral Ritual
Yoga · Qigong · Pranayama · Bowls · Varessence

Begin standing and fold forward over your toes, knees softly bent, swaying the back of the body gently side to side.

Move to the floor, sit tall, do slow then fast spinal twists, release into a forward fold, open the legs wide, come into Child's Pose, and close in Savasana for at least five minutes — not sleep, conscious release.

Move into Astral Qigong to dissolve any remaining tension. Watch Astral Qigong →

Pull your daily mudra card and hold the gesture, reflecting on the day's activities until those thoughts are no longer prominent.

Write it down. What happened, what you felt, what lingered — not a to-do list, a what-I've-done and what-I've-noticed list.

If there was anything difficult about the day, note it — and note one small thing that could shift it tomorrow. Writing it down is an act of release.

Form Vishnu Mudra. Chandra Bhedana — the left-nostril lunar breath — inhale left, exhale right, for 5–10 rounds. For an even softer ending, transition into Bhramari (humming bee breath).

Close the practice with your singing bowls — begin with the lowest note and climb to the highest, the reverse of the morning sequence. When the last tone fades completely, the field is clear.

Hands together at the heart center. Speak the Astral Varessence aloud to seal the day before sleep.

"Gratitude fills my heart, I've done my part. I send love from the end to the start."
