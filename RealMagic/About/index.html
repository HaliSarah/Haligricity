About the Book

Real Magic: An Introduction was written by Hali Sarah Parsons and published on February 10, 2017. A personal growth workbook designed to be lived in, not just read.

It introduces the foundational principles of what would become Haligricity — a synthesis of global wisdom traditions, energy practices, and practical tools for conscious living. The Living Way before it had a name.

About the Author

Hali Sarah Parsons is the founder of I.Q. Productionz and the creator of Haligricity. Also author of Real Magic: Book Two — Advanced Practice (2026).
